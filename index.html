One widget, any brand
Colour, type, corners, names and voice are tokens. A new store goes live by filling in its brand kit, not by redesigning screens.

    
Nothing is bought without a tap
The assistant prepares the order, but the shopper confirms the total, address and card before anything is charged.

    
It recovers instead of failing
When a colour is sold out, it offers what ships today or the restock date, never a dead end.

    
A person is one tap away
"Talk to a person" stays in the header, and the team sees the whole conversation.
